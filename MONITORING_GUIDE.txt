┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  🎯 COMPLETE DEMO SCRIPT                                       │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

  Step 1: Open Terminal 1 and run:
  
  docker-compose logs -f remediation_engine


  Step 2: Open Terminal 2 and run:
  
  # Inject a crash fault
  curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/inject/500_checkout
  
  # Generate error traffic
  for ($i=1; $i -le 30; $i++) {
      curl -s -X POST http://localhost:8000/checkout
      Start-Sleep -Milliseconds 100
  }


  Step 3: Watch Terminal 1
  
  You'll see the AI detect, decide, and heal!


  Step 4: Open Browser
  
  http://localhost:8000/dashboard
  
  Watch the dashboard update in real-time!

# 📄 How to Generate Your IEEE Research Paper (PDF)

To ensure your paper is of **publication quality (IEEE Standard)**, I have provided two professional ways to generate the final PDF.

---

## Option 1: The Gold Standard (Overleaf / LaTeX)
This is the only method accepted by IEEE journals. It uses the official `IEEEtran` class.

1.  **Open [Overleaf.com](https://www.overleaf.com)** (Free online LaTeX editor).
2.  Create a **New Project** -> **Blank Project**.
3.  Copy the entire content from the file I created: `IEEE_Paper_Source.tex`.
4.  Paste it into the `main.tex` file in Overleaf.
5.  Click **Recompile** -> **Download PDF**.

**Result**: A perfect, two-column, academically formatted IEEE research paper.

---

## Option 2: Instant Preview (HTML to PDF)
Use this if you need a quick version to show your professor right now.

1.  Locate the file `IEEE_Paper_Preview.html` in your project folder.
2.  Open it in **Google Chrome** or **Edge**.
3.  Press **Ctrl + P** (Print).
4.  Select **"Save as PDF"** in the printer destination.
5.  *Tip: Ensure "Background Graphics" is checked for the best look.*
